# Snap a chalk line between two marks: pull it taut, pluck it straight up

A chalk line is hooked on a nail at one mark on a board. One arm carries the reel to the other mark and holds the line taut. The other arm pinches the line at its middle, lifts it straight up and lets go, and the snap leaves a white chalk line between the marks. The reel then goes back on its rest.

**Ability tested:** keeping a line taut with one arm while the other lifts and releases it, so that the snap leaves a straight mark.

**Both arms are forced by layout.** The line prints only when taut, which needs a gripper closed on the reel at the near mark, and the middle of the line is 30 cm from there, against the gripper's 9.6 cm opening. A reel left on the board leaves the line slack, and letting go of a slack line prints it bowed. This is reasoned, and has not been run in physics.

## Requirements

`yam-isaac-lab-arena` with Isaac Lab-Arena 0.3.1 (`source <ARENA_DIR>/yam.env`). `mechkit.py` and `demokit.py` are in this folder. Importing `mechkit` hooks `yam_arena.scene` and `yam_arena.inspect_embodiment` without changing them on disk.

## Run a trial

```bash
inspect-robots run --embodiment yam_arena -E task=task.py -E object_jitter=0 -E control_hz=20 \
  -E cam_width=320 -E cam_height=176 --scorer task_score --max-steps 1200 \
  --instruction "Snap a chalk line between the two marks. The hook is on the far nail. Pull the reel to the near mark and hold the line taut across both marks, then lift the line straight up at its middle and let it go. Put the reel on its rest."
```

## Scripted demo

```bash
python demokit.py task.py OUT            # full score, with a video
python3 check_score.py                   # the score lines on hand-built states, with no simulator
```

The demo reads the reel's pose, as a camera could see it, and the marks' known places.

## Runtime and seeds

The demo takes about 20 s of simulated time, about 1.5 minutes of wall time on one GPU. There is no randomness: `object_jitter=0`, so no seed is needed.
