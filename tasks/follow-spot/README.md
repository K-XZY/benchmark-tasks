# Keep a moving head's camera on a moving figure, and stop at the lamp

A camera on a moving head is panned by its long bar to follow a small person on a belt rail, which moves and stops as the seed says. When a red lamp comes on, a button must be pressed within 1 s with the camera still on the figure.

**Ability tested:** keeping aim on a target that moves unpredictably, in real time, through a tool, then reacting to a cue with the other arm without losing aim.

**Both arms are needed only for the press credit.** One arm can track. An arm that leaves the bar for the button loses the aim, as the figure moves out of the band in under a second, and the right arm cannot reach the button at all. This is reasoned, and has not been run in physics.

## Requirements

`yam-isaac-lab-arena` with Isaac Lab-Arena 0.3.1 (`source <ARENA_DIR>/yam.env`). `mechkit.py` and `demokit.py` are in this folder. Importing `mechkit` hooks `yam_arena.scene` and `yam_arena.inspect_embodiment` without changing them on disk.

## Run a trial

```bash
TASK_SEED=1 inspect-robots run --embodiment yam_arena -E task=task.py -E object_jitter=0 -E control_hz=20 \
  -E cam_width=320 -E cam_height=176 --scorer task_score --max-steps 1000 \
  --instruction "Hold the bar of the moving head and turn it so that the camera stays on the figure as it moves along its rail. When the red lamp comes on, press the stop button within 1 s while keeping the camera on the figure. When the figure has stopped, let go of the bar."
```

## Scripted demo

```bash
python demokit.py task.py OUT            # full score, with a video
python3 check_score.py                   # the score lines on hand-built states, and plans that do not read the figure, with no simulator
```

The demo reads the figure's position and the bar's angle, as a camera could see them, and the lamp from the top camera's picture. It never reads the belt's schedule. `TASK_DEMO` selects another mode: `none`, `fixed`, `sweep`, `lag`, `late`, `early`, `no_press`, `dash`, `left_dash`, `hold_on`, each with an optional `@seed`.

## Runtime and seeds

The demo takes about 38 s of simulated time, about 2.5 minutes of wall time on one GPU. The seed is the environment variable `TASK_SEED`, default 1.
